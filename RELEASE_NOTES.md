## Downloads

- `SoraMod-0.4.1-win-x64.zip`: GTA V Enhanced Story Mode trainer, including the ASI plugin, 13 languages and bundled fonts.

## New Features and Bug Fixes

- Added invincibility, health and armor restoration, wanted level removal, ammunition refill, vehicle repair and engine restart.
- Added support for Online-exclusive vehicles and spawning by model name.
- Added persistent pinned vehicles, with remote unpinning, teleporting into vehicles and map waypoints.
- Added map waypoint teleportation, time and weather controls.
- Added keyboard, mouse and controller support.

## System Requirements and Dependencies

- 64-bit Windows 10 or later and GTA V Enhanced.
- A game-compatible version of [Script Hook V](https://www.dev-c.com/gtav/scripthookv/) and an ASI Loader, installed separately.
- Disable BattlEye. Story Mode only.
